Oscillator interaction & modulation

Contextual settings for features without dedicated controls on the approved panel.

SHIFT-click, double-click or long-press UP selects UP/DOWN arpeggiation. Chorus I and II can be enabled simultaneously by clicking both buttons. Pitch wheel springs to centre. MIDI: notes, pitch bend, CC1 modulation, CC64 sustain, CC120/123 panic. Patch changes stop notes and transports. Clicking SF-POLY opens this panel.
